# 13 - Design System & Theming Guidelines

## Design Principles
1. **Restrained, Premium & Cinematic Aesthetic**:
   - Deep slate/obsidian dark themes (`#0A0D14`, `#121620`, `#1A202C`) with refined subtle accents (`#6366F1`, `#38BDF8`, `#10B981`).
   - Avoid garish neon gradients or superfluous visual noise.
2. **Design Tokens**:
   - Define centralized tokens for:
     - **Colors**: Primary, Secondary, Background, Surface, SurfaceVariant, Error, Success, Warning, DiffAdd, DiffDelete.
     - **Typography**: Display, Headline, Title, Body, CodeMono (JetBrains Mono / Fira Code).
     - **Spacing**: 4dp grid (4dp, 8dp, 12dp, 16dp, 24dp, 32dp).
     - **Elevation & Radius**: Crisp rounded corners (8dp for chips, 12dp for cards, 16dp for modal sheets).
